Research Profile

I study how learning systems should behave when the world they run in does not hold still. My main line of work is nested, multi-timescale learning: letting different parts of a model update at different rates, which I have developed for federated learning, multi-agent reinforcement learning in 6G networks and adaptive-fidelity inference. A second line concerns language-model agents: when a reasoning model should stop thinking, how cheap guards can supervise scoped agents, and how agentic interfaces move risk rather than remove it. Across both I value results that survive honest testing, including negative ones: my MSc dissertation shows that no published early-exit halting signal generalises across domains, and explains why. I want to do open-ended, conceptually ambitious AI research in a collaborative group, drawing on applied work in wireless networks, healthcare, power systems and security.

Interests: multi-timescale and nested learning · LLM agents and reasoning · multi-agent reinforcement learning · federated and privacy-preserving learning · trustworthy, reproducible machine learning.

Record: 14 peer-reviewed papers published or accepted (2 journal, 12 conference; 8 as first author) · 10 under review · Best Paper Award, ICSL-DSGA 2026 · 3 co-supervised undergraduate projects produced accepted papers.
